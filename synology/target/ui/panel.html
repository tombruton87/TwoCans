    Network and region

    Where phones find twocans. If the Synology's address changes, put the new one here.
    Bedtime and call hours follow it — like Europe/London.
    twocans restarts with these; the phones' and phone line's ports are kept.

    HTTPS

    twocans can use one of this Synology's own certificates — like the Let's Encrypt one DSM keeps for your DDNS name — and follow it through DSM's renewals. Face ID sign-in and adding twocans to a phone's home screen need one. To make one, go to Control Panel → Security → Certificate → Add.

    Looking after twocans

    Start parts again when they stop workingA part that's stopped, or stopped answering, for a minute and a half is started again. After three times in an hour, it's left for you to look at.
    Tell DSM when something needs youIn DSM's notifications: a part that stopped, setup that didn't finish, the phone line dropping, a new release.
    …and when a phone's been offline for a daySo an unplugged phone doesn't go unnoticed.

    Who else can look

    DSM users who aren't administrators can see this window — how twocans is, its calls and its numbers — but change nothing. Administrators always can.

    Separated by commas. In Control Panel → Application Privileges, they also need to be allowed twocans.

    Your data

    Everything twocans holds, in one zip: the call log, contacts and voicemails as spreadsheets, and the recordings and voicemails as audio. It holds your family's voices, so keep it somewhere private.
